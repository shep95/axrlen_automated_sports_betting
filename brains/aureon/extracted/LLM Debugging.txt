`TS2322`).
     *    Aureon  then  queries  the  LSP  for  "Go  to  Definition"  of  the  variables  involved.      *    It  bundles  this  (Error  +  Definition  +  Usage)  into  the  prompt.   ####  B.  THE  "RUNTIME"  HOOK  (THE  CRASH  REPORT)  *    **The  Tech:**  When  the  app  crashes,  it  dumps  a  stack  trace  to  the  terminal.  *    **The  Hack:**  Aureon  watches  the  terminal  output.      *    **Regex  Trigger:**  If  it  sees  `Error:`,  `Exception`,  or  `Panic`:      *    **Action:**  It  parses  the  filename  and  line  number  (e.g.,  `main.py:45`).      *    **Auto-Read:**  It  reads  `main.py`  lines  30-60  automatically.      *    **Auto-Prompt:**  "I  detected  a  crash  at  line  45.  Here  is  the  context.  Fix  it."   ####  C.  THE  "REFLECTION"  LOOP  (CHAIN  OF  THOUGHT)  *    **The  Prompt  Engineering:**  Do  not  ask  "How  to  fix  this?"  *    **The  Elite  Prompt:**      ```text      SYSTEM:  You  are  a  Senior  Principal  Engineer  at  Google.
