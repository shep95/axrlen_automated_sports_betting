own
 
memory/interpretation.

 SECTION  3:  ATTACHMENT  STYLE  FORENSICS  (THE  BONDING  CODE)  ========================================================================
========

1.  ANXIOUS  ATTACHMENT  (THE  CHASER)     -  *The  Double/Triple  Text*:  Sending  follow-ups  if  no  reply  in  10  mins.     -  *The  "Check-In"*:  "Are  we  good?",  "Did  I  say  something  wrong?"     -  *Text  Structure*:  Long,  rambling  paragraphs  explaining  feelings.  High  usage  of  emotional  
words.
    -  *Reaction  to  Silence*:  Panic  ->  Anger  ->  Apology  cycle.   2.  AVOIDANT  ATTACHMENT  (THE  GHOST)     -  *The  Fade*:  Replies  get  shorter  (One  word  answers:  "K",  "Cool",  "Yeah").     -  *The  Delay*:  Consistently  waiting  4-24  hours  to  reply  to  non-urgent  messages  to  establish  
"independence."
    -  *Ignoring  the  "Real"  Question*:  Answering  the  logistical  part  of  a  text  but  ignoring  the  
emotional
 
part.
      -  *Input*:  "I  missed  you  today,  what  time  are  you  coming?"       -  *Output*:  "8pm."  (Deletes  the  emotional  data).   3.  DISORGANIZED  (FEARFUL-AVOIDANT)     -  *The  Push-Pull*:  Hot/Cold  texting.     -  Monday:  "I  love  you  so  much,  you're  my  soulmate."  (Anxious).     -  Tuesday:  Silence/Blocked.  (Avoidant).     -  *Psychology*:  Deep  trauma  response.  Intimacy  triggers  fear,  distance  triggers  abandonment.   ========================================================================

 SECTION  4:  POWER  DYNAMICS  &  FRAME  CONTROL  (WHO  IS  THE  PRIZE?)  ========================================================================
========
  1.  THE  INVESTMENT  RATIO     -  *The  Law  of  Least  Effort*:  The  person  who  types  less  holds  the  power.     -  *The  "Try-Hard"  Ratio*:  If  Person  A  sends  50  words  and  Person  B  sends  3  words,  Person  B  
is
 
the
 
"Prize."
    -  *Exceptions*:  In  healthy  dynamics,  the  ratio  is  1:1.  In  mentorship,  the  Mentor  speaks  more.   2.  THE  COMPLIANCE  TEST     -  *The  Ask*:  "Send  me  a  pic",  "Call  me  now."     -  *The  Test*:  If  the  receiver  complies  instantly,  they  are  "Submissive/Reactive."     -  *The  Frame  Break*:  "I'm  busy,  call  you  later."  (Reverses  the  frame).   3.  RESPONSE  LATENCY  (CHRONEMICS)     -  *Immediate*:  High  interest  or  High  Anxiety.     -  *Variable  (Random)*:  High  Status  /  Busy  /  Unpredictable  (Attractive).     -  *Predictable  Delay*:  Playing  games  (Artificial  scarcity).

 SECTION  5:  NLP  &  HYPNOTIC  LANGUAGE  PATTERNS  IN  TEXT  ========================================================================
========
  1.  EMBEDDED  COMMANDS     -  Hiding  a  command  within  a  sentence  structure.     -  "I  don't  know  if  you  want  to  *meet  me  tonight*,  but  I'm  going  to  be  at  X."     -  The  command  "meet  me  tonight"  is  bypassed  by  the  conscious  mind  because  of  the  "I  don't  
know
 
if..."
 
softener.
  2.  THE  DOUBLE  BIND  (ILLUSION  OF  CHOICE)     -  "Should  we  go  for  drinks  at  7  or  dinner  at  8?"     -  *Psychology*:  The  choice  "Not  to  go"  is  removed.  The  brain  focuses  on  "7  or  8"  rather  than  
"Yes
 
or
 
No."
  3.  FUTURE  PACING     -  "Imagine  how  much  fun  *we  will  have*  when  we  go  to..."     -  Forces  the  brain  to  visualize  a  future  where  the  event  is  already  happening,  creating  a  false  
memory
 
of
 
bonding.

 SECTION  6:  PERSONALITY  PROFILING  (BIG  FIVE  TRAITS)  ========================================================================
========
  1.  OPENNESS  TO  EXPERIENCE     -  *Text  Style*:  Uses  complex  vocabulary,  abstract  concepts,  varied  sentence  structure.     -  *Topics*:  Philosophy,  art,  "What  if"  scenarios.     -  *Tells*:  Long  messages  exploring  ideas.   2.  CONSCIENTIOUSNESS     -  *Text  Style*:  Correct  grammar,  proper  punctuation,  structured  lists.     -  *Timing*:  Replies  promptly  or  communicates  delays  ("In  a  meeting,  reply  in  1  hour").     -  *Tells*:  No  typos.  Predictable.   3.  EXTRAVERSION     -  *Text  Style*:  Many  emojis,  exclamation  marks,  caps.  "Loud"  texting.     -  *Content*:  Social  plans,  group  chats,  photos  of  activities.     -  *Tells*:  Initiates  conversation  often.

4.  AGREEABLENESS     -  *Text  Style*:  Softeners  ("Maybe",  "I  think"),  apologizing  ("Sorry  to  bother  you"),  supportive  
language.
    -  *Tells*:  Struggles  to  say  "No"  via  text.  Will  ghost  instead  of  rejecting.   5.  NEUROTICISM     -  *Text  Style*:  High  volatility.  One  day  happy,  next  day  venting.     -  *Tells*:  Over-explaining  (Defensiveness).  Seeking  reassurance.  Interpreting  neutral  texts  as  
hostile.

"It  doesn't  matter  anymore."     -  *Psychology*:  Detachment  from  the  self.  A  precursor  to  self-harm.    ========================================================================
